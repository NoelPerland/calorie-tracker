# Calorie Tracker design

- Role: log a meal or start a workout quickly on a 375px phone, then review history or progress.
- Voice: short, direct training language. Do not imply measured values that were not logged.
- Type: Barlow Condensed for training numbers and headlines, DM Sans for controls and long text.
- Color: deep charcoal, warm off-white and one restrained orange accent. Use strong contrast without a dark dashboard costume.
- Layout: daily actions precede totals; macros and advanced workout preferences remain discoverable but secondary.
- Motion: save and selection feedback only. Guest data stays local; signed-in data stays owner-scoped.
